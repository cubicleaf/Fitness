HOW IT GOES

What to expect.
1
You send the details

The form is the best way in — it gives me the car, the address, and the package up front, so when I call to confirm I already know it’s a job I can take. Call or text if you’d rather.

2
I show up with everything

Products, vacuum, power — it’s all in my car. I just need to reach your outdoor spigot for water.

3
We walk the car together

I photograph existing wear before I start, so we both know exactly where we began.

4
You inspect, then pay

You look it over with me before I leave. If I missed something, I sort it out then. Payment comes after.

WHO SHOWS UP

Tim Carson

My name is Tim Carson. I spent my 20s teaching abroad, but I decided to leave education and now I am working for myself.

I have lived around Cleveland for two years and I’ve grown to appreciate Tennessee life: the pace, the nature, the people. I’ve known for a few years that I had to end up working for myself, and now that is reality.

I’ve always enjoyed the process of cleaning. It is inherently valuable, and while it is not appreciated by everyone, it is a worthwhile use of time. Right now I focus on careful, thorough cleaning — the kind of work that quietly makes a car feel new again. I am building up my systems and best practices as time goes on and one day I will be offering ceramic coatings and deep cleans, etc. But for now I’m starting at the beginning and doing a great job at the simple things I already know how to do and I’m improving the operations every day.

Thanks for dropping in. I look forward to talking about your car and how we can team up against entropy.
