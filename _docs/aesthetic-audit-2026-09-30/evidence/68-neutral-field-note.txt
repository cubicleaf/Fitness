What is a neutral activity?

Neutral activities are for things you want to record without pretending they are a normal lift. They still keep feel, notes, history, and edit, but they skip the set logging controls.

Use this for note-only events, recovery items, or anything where “it happened” matters more than weight, reps, or time.
