FIELD NOTE
What does grip tracking do?

When this is on, the first set screen can show optional grip choices for that activity. Once you pick one for that activity on that day, the app silently reuses it for later sets instead of asking again.

This is best for movements where hand position materially changes the feel of the exercise, like pulldowns, rows, curls, carries, or hanging work.
