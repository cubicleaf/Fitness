Abstract text-bed for a literary essay channel: a page edge and the shadow it throws, rendered as worn paper edge. Lit by a single low rim from the left. Colour is restricted to deep burgundy #4A1525, dark mauve #2D1020 and near-black #0A0506, with muted rose #4A2035 only in transitions and a single thread of gold #D4A853 as an edge or hairline — gold is never a fill. Composition leaves the left half almost empty as a text-safe zone: no detail, no focal point, no subject there. Quiet, low contrast, generous dead space, matte and slightly grained. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no lens flare, no dramatic spectacle, no neon, no orange. This is a bed for words, not a picture. Aspect ratio 16:9.
