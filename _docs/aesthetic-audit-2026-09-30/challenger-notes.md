## Overall judgment

The research rubric already avoids most overreach. The defensible result is a refinement of the current warm plum/mauve identity, not a return to hollow controls or a gold/cream redesign. The September 30 decisions are explicit user choices, not assumptions inferred from existing code (STATUS.md:61–65). Their authority exceeds older generic playbook examples.

## Challenges and alternative readings

1. **Filled mauve repetition can be coherent rather than over-emphasis.** A keypad or set-action group is a repeated family; filling its members establishes rhythm and a common action language. Do not mark “too much accent” merely because several controls share mauve. The useful question is whether the selected/current control and primary data remain distinguishable in the actual composition. The final CSS at index.html:2229–2260 deliberately uses 34% and 22% mauve mixes. Do not recommend stripping these fills as generic clutter reduction.

2. **Borderless is an intended silhouette.** Low contrast between a sheet and its background is not automatically a failure if the scrim, content grouping and placement establish the layer. WCAG non-text contrast does not require every edge to be 3:1. Any proposal to add borders must be rejected unless an essential visual cue is demonstrably missing and a compatible tonal repair is insufficient. Latest STATUS explicitly removes outer modal borders.

3. **An 11px floor and contrast are separate evidence.** The documented floor can establish an internal inconsistency even if a 10px label happens to pass contrast; a readable 11px label still fails if its effective contrast is too low. Avoid presenting enlargement alone as the fix. Preserve compactness and test wrapping. Conversely do not call every low-opacity item a failure: disabled controls and redundant/decorative detail require proper classification first.

4. **Gold, white and black are role/context decisions.** Gold artwork on the mauve Add Activity plate is confirmed direction, not a mandate to make every icon gold. Pure white selected text can serve a needed luminance role. Near-black shadows are explicitly allowed by the playbook’s dark-surface corollary. Slightly different gold hexes and a black shadow literal are not perceptual defects by themselves.

5. **Cool note tags are a provisional mismatch, not proof of broken identity.** The reference memo correctly locates #7b93f5 at index.html:12529. An alternative reading is that tags are a deliberate categorical cue. That reading currently lacks documented support, and equivalent tag treatments elsewhere would decide the issue. Label it a source-confirmed outlier until the branch is reached and its role compared; do not elevate it to a top visible finding if unseen. A #555 handle is similarly too small and nonsemantic to outrank faint workout information.

6. **Supplied icon construction is part of the identity.** A filled scale, stroked lock and custom person can belong together through color, optical size and placement. Identical stroke widths are not the objective. Preserve custom artwork and judge paired daily/history uses directly. A second visible plate around an already self-contained icon is a stronger aesthetic concern than different SVG construction techniques.

7. **The reference projects demonstrate a family resemblance, not a stencil.** HF’s strict gold-punctuation rule and Carson’s gold-filled booking controls disagree legitimately because their jobs differ. Fitness inherits warmth, deliberate emphasis and careful proportions; it need not inherit identical hexes, serif display typography, paper sections, texture, atmospheric effects or marketing spacing. The system sans supports the utility’s brief reading intervals.
